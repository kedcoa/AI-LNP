The remaining work is to run experiment-scoped extraction when a suitable

The progression was:

1. **Initial extraction schema**

   The LLM read abstracts and filled fields such as formulation, component, payload, recipient cell, therapeutic target, experiment context, and outcome. Pydantic checked types, required fields, allowed values, and structural consistency.

2. **More comprehensive extraction schema**

   Human review exposed deeper problems: fields from separate experiments were mixed, delivery recipients were confused with therapeutic targets, cells and outcomes were merged, and valid-looking values lacked appropriate evidence. Therefore, the flat schema became an experiment-scoped evidence graph containing:

   - Explicit experiment IDs
   - Atomic LNP components
   - Separate payload and encoded-product entities
   - Separate recipient-cell and therapeutic-target relations
   - Separate endpoints and outcome values
   - Exact evidence quotations
   - Typed relationships between entities
   - A second-reader correction pass

3. **Full-text RAG**

   Even the improved schema could not recover information absent from an abstract. RAG was added to search full text and supplements for the relevant evidence before extraction. It improves what information reaches the extractor; it does not replace the schema.

So the final design combines both:

```text
Full-text RAG
    finds the relevant evidence
            ↓
Experiment-scoped evidence graph
    organizes the evidence correctly
            ↓
Validators and second reader
    reject structural and evidentiary errors
            ↓
Human scientific verification
    determines whether the result is actually correct
```
